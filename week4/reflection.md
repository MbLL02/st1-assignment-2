Prior to working with AI, I created a procedural Python program with lists, dictionaries, and validations to store and print appointment data. Although the program is working fine for usual cases, there were a number of problems associated with its lack of persistence and the absence of preventing duplicate time slots. The help from Microsoft Copilot, in turn, made me notice some structural errors in the way I was developing, for instance, validation of whitespace-only input, or date strings instead of just checking if values are truthy. Nevertheless, the suggestions from the AI contained a number of unnecessary assumptions, such as auto-incrementing IDs, strict ISO formatting for dates and times, and extra status flags that were not required in our case at Stage 1. I tested the code generated by the AI myself by running it on my terminal and inputting some edge cases such as None, empty strings, and duplicates. Thus, the above experience proved that certain software engineering tasks cannot be performed by the AI.
