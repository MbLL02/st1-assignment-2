When the AI assistant implemented Stage 4, it tried to inject external dependencies to the domain layer, namely inserting SQL commands within Appointment.cancel() and introducing a fictional NotificationService class. I refused both ideas since domain classes are supposed to reflect business logic rather than infrastructure and notification pipelines are totally unrelated to the initial brief about an early prototype of community clinic. Nevertheless, I agreed to use Python's Enum for the AppointmentStatus as it was better than primitive strings due to having compile-time state constraints and avoiding typos within strings. It was crucial to have the validated UML diagram at the stage of the previous work, because it provided an engineering contract that forced the AI assistant to implement only required attributes and associations and did not allow inventing various sub-classes or managers.
